# Direction artistique, série 2 : des intérieurs comme chez les gens

La série 1 est belle mais trop idéale : elle reste pour les ambiances et la page Inspirations. La série 2 montre **des logements ordinaires** (studio, appartement des années 70, pavillon, maison de village, location) et **ce que le covering y change vraiment**.

## Les règles

- **On part de l'avant**, comme sur un vrai chantier : une pièce ordinaire, lumière du quotidien, quelques objets de tous les jours. Puis on en tire **trois après** par édition, chacun dans de vraies références du catalogue (la vignette réelle est donnée au modèle). Seules les surfaces couvertes changent.
- **Façades planes uniquement** : pas de portes à moulures, de carrelage de plan de travail ni de crédence à joints, que le covering ne rend pas parfaitement.
- **Trois variantes par pièce** : une neutre, une bois, une couleur. C'est la démonstration de la personnalisation.
- **Teintes vérifiées** (ΔE 2000 ≤ 12 après balance des blancs), sinon on réétiquette vers la référence la plus proche, comme pour la série 1.
- **Étiquettes** : « Ambiance » pour toutes ces images, « Exemple » pour les repères du simulateur.

## Volume

122 images, 256 essais, environ 10 $ (plafond 20 $).

## Les prompts

Ils sont tous dans `scripts/photos-serie-2.json` (CRM). Styles communs :

Avant :
```
Realistic photograph of an ordinary, lived-in French home, the kind of room most people actually live in. Taken by a real-estate photographer with a full-frame camera and a 24 mm lens at about 1.5 m height, perfectly straight verticals, natural perspective, no fisheye. Ordinary daylight from the existing window mixed with the room's own ceiling light, true-to-life neutral colours, no colour grading, everyday exposure. Average-sized room with standard-quality fittings: ordinary ceiling, white sockets and switches, a radiator, PVC or painted wooden windows with a roller-shutter box. Clean and tidy but genuinely lived-in: a few everyday objects (a kettle, a tea towel, a fruit bowl, a plant, a dish rack), nothing staged, nothing luxurious, no designer furniture. Photorealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph. No people, no text, no readable labels, no logos, no brand names, no watermark. Avoid: magazine styling, dramatic light, CGI or 3D-render look, plastic-looking surfaces, oversaturated colours, HDR halos, bent or wavy lines, duplicated or floating handles, dirt or mess.
```
Après (édition, avec les vignettes réelles en images 2, 3…) :
```
Edit this exact photo, the first image. Keep everything identical: camera position, framing, light, shadows, walls, floor, ceiling, window, {garder}, appliances, sink, taps, handles and every object. Change only these surfaces: {surfaces}. Each new surface reproduces its material sample exactly (colour, pattern, grain direction and texture at a realistic scale), as a premium self-adhesive architectural film applied over the existing panels: perfectly smooth, tight wrapped edges, realistic sheen, following the existing light, shadows and reflections. Door and drawer shapes, gaps, edges and handles stay exactly the same. No other change.
```
Repères :
```
Plain reference photo of an ordinary, typical French {sujet}, taken straight on, facing the units, from the middle of the room, camera at 1.4 m height with a 26 mm smartphone-like perspective, perfectly straight verticals. The whole element is visible from the floor to its top with a small margin around it, nothing hides the fronts, almost nothing on the worktop. Even, soft daylight, neutral true-to-life colours, no grading. Ordinary room, standard fittings, honest and simple. Photorealistic, like a good photo a homeowner would take to ask for a quote. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark. Avoid: decoration, styling, dramatic light, CGI look, distortion, bent lines, duplicated or floating handles.
```
Ambiances accessibles :
```
Bright, honest interior photograph of a recently refreshed ordinary French apartment or house: attainable, mid-range, real proportions (a small to average room), standard appliances, ordinary ceiling, radiator, PVC or wooden windows, white sockets and switches. Taken with a full-frame camera and a {lens} lens at eye level, straight verticals, natural daylight from the window, a light warm grade. Tidy and pleasant with a few everyday objects, nothing luxurious, no designer pieces. The renovated surfaces are flat panels covered with a premium self-adhesive architectural film: perfectly smooth, tight clean edges, realistic sheen and texture. Photorealistic, indistinguishable from a real photograph. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark. Avoid: luxury magazine look, oversized rooms, CGI look, plastic surfaces, oversaturation, bent lines, duplicated or floating handles.
```
